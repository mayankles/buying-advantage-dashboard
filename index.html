A negative advantage means renting the same home and investing your down payment, closing costs, and monthly savings would have left you wealthier. Buying starts roughly ten percent underwater — the round-trip cost of transacting — and climbs out through equity and appreciation.
How the refinance assumption works Each scenario's refi rate is your mortgage rate plus its offset. If that lands more than your refi sensitivity below your locked rate, the model refinances at the start of year 2, re-amortizing the remaining balance over the remaining original term. The payment savings get invested.
